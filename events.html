The Events of Greensboro

A City full of life

Greensboro has a special position as the center of North Carolina, at the perfect spot for concerts, conventions, festivals and more! There are several places in greensboro to go and see some pretty great things. The following information will go over the most popular events of each category, some information about the biggest venue's in the city, and where you can go do plan your trip now!

We start with the Greensboro Folk Festival, the biggest annual festival in Greensboro. It takes place in the fall of each year. More details are available on the offical website.

Sports

First National Bank Field and First Horizon Coliseum host major sporting events for the Greensboro Grasshoppers and Greensboro Gargoyles.

Greensboro Grasshoppers

The Greensboro Grasshoppers play at First National Bank Field as a minor leauge team affiliated with the Pittsburgh Pirates. The stadium features open seating, firework shows during the summer, and countless events to spice up America's pastime!

Greensboro Gargoyles

The Greensboro Gargoyles are fresh on the scene, forming in 2025. They are affiliated with the Carolina Hurricanes. Get in on the ground floor for this fresh team and enjoy the amenities at First Horizon Coliseum!

More Community Events

Downtown in December

Downtown in December celebrates the winter season with a festival of lights, ice skating rink, and Holiday Parade.

Winter Wonderlights

Greensboro Science Center presents a light show with over a million lights, handcrafted lanterns, and signature glowing tunnel.

First Friday

The first friday of each month, gather downtown and experience an open air celebration of local culture and community spirit.
