The Revitalized downtown

A Humble Downtown to Remember

Greensboro's revitalized downtown has so much to offer for any kind of traveler. From local coffee shops and retail stores, to the brands you know and love, downtown Greensboro is the bustling heart of the city.

Albeit a far cry from the constant hustle and bustle of downtown life in heavily populated cities, Greensboro offers a more relaxed and local feeling downtown experience, allowing you to connect more with the community.

The following lists are of the highest rated, most popular, and well known spots to visit downtown.

Coffee Shops

  • Northern Roots

    Women-owned cafe serving organic coffee and espresso, free Wi-Fi, free seating, and gender-neutral restrooms.

  • Awoo Coffee

    A comfortable and dog friendly coffee shop serving specialty coffee and homemade syrups.

  • Common Grounds

    Relaxed coffee shop and bar featuring creative espresso drinks, live music, an arcade, and pool table.

Restaurants

  • Cheesecakes by Alex

    One of the biggest staples of downtown Greensboro, Cheesecakes by Alex offers premium cheesecake freshly homemade alongside ice cream, coffee, and free WI-FI.

  • Bourbon Bowl

    Locally-owned and operated bowling alley with 6 full-service bowling lanes, a cocktail bar and indoor/outdoor dining areas.

  • Boxcar Bar + Arcade

    Lively arcade bar featuring a wide selection of vintage and modern games alongside craft drinks and pizza.

  • BonChon

    Casual chain serving cooked-to-order Korean fried chicken, plus other traditional eats.

  • Mellow Mushroom

    A fresh stone-baked pizza restaurant featuring a cathedral-inspired interior, dozens of creative pizza options and 32 draft beers.

Shopping

  • Hype Clinic

    A locally owned and run streetwear and sneaker shop specializing in authentic footwear, apparel, and accessories.

  • Pop Vintage & Collectable

    A wacky array of authentic vintage collectables for the nerds, featuring toys, comics, plushies, and more.

  • Lee | Wrangler

    Two of the most iconic denim brands under one roof. Home to a broad collection of clothing trusted for its comfort, quality, and style.

  • Stolen Skate Shop

    Skater-owned storefront for skateboards, shoes, and apparel with knowledgeable staff and helpful service.
